# Fix the ledger, then wire Kenya's rails

Ledger Link should spend its next sprint making its own numbers right, not adding features. A code audit of commit `144ee4a` (28 September 2026) found two **critical** defects that produce wrong figures without raising any error: bank matching posts to receivables and payables without settling the underlying invoice or bill, and unpaginated reads silently stop at PostgREST's row cap. Around them sit an accountant role the database refuses to let post, no period lock, a base currency that can be changed after posting, a dashboard that counts void invoices as money owed, payroll that silently drops allowances, and an app shell served with no Content-Security-Policy or clickjacking protection. Most are one-to-five-day fixes, and each undercuts PRODUCT.md's own test of success, "a finance director trusting the numbers." Once the books are right, the evidence sets a clear order: first close the Kenyan table-stakes gaps (M-Pesa and bank statement import, credit notes and estimates, payroll that applies the reliefs the Finance Act 2025 made mandatory, and eTIMS), then compete where rivals are thin, on purchase-side eTIMS evidence (KRA has checked income-tax-return expenses against eTIMS since 1 January 2026), live M-Pesa through Daraja with STK pay links, bundled statutory payroll, and month-end tooling for accountants who serve many clients. eTIMS alone will not set Ledger Link apart, because Zoho Books Kenya already pairs KRA-approved eTIMS and native M-Pesa with a free tier and paid plans from about KES 849 a month, and at least six local startups advertise the same headline. Almost every external figure here came from search-engine summaries because the research sessions could not fetch full pages, so the statutory rates, deadline rules and prices flagged in the final section are provisional until checked against KRA, Kenya Law, Safaricom and vendor primary sources.

## Two critical defects let the ledger disagree with itself

The core is sound, which is why the defects at its edges matter so much. `private.insert_journal_entry` requires at least two lines of non-negative integer cents with exactly one non-zero side, checks that every account is active and belongs to the organisation, and raises on any unbalanced or zero-value entry (`supabase/migrations/20260917172909_add_atomic_financial_workflows.sql:110-218`). Invoice, bill, payment and payroll RPCs require idempotency keys taken under `pg_advisory_xact_lock`, reject replays with changed parameters, and write their audit rows in the same transaction (`…atomic_financial_workflows.sql:374-387, 859-888, 1453-1465`). The audit found no route that reads or writes another tenant's rows. The failures sit where money enters or leaves that core, and two are rated critical because they produce plausible-looking wrong numbers rather than errors.

**The general ledger and the AR/AP subledgers drift apart through three doors.** `BankingService.matchTransaction` posts a bank line against whatever account the matcher picks, including receivables 1100 or payables 2000, but never applies the payment to the invoice or bill, so `amount_due_cents` and the customer balance stay open while the ledger says the debt is settled (`src/server/banking.ts:383-420`). Manual journals can post straight to those control accounts because `post_journal_entry` does not block them (`…atomic_financial_workflows.sql:281-294`). And there is no way back from a payment: no reverse-payment RPC exists, and voiding a paid invoice is refused with "reverse its payments first" (`…atomic_financial_workflows.sql:1246-1252`), a dead end. The matcher compounds the damage. What the interface calls AI matching is **keyword heuristics with invented confidence scores** (any description containing "TOTAL" becomes fuel expense at 95%, "M-PESA" becomes sales revenue at 94%), and auto-reconcile posts anything scoring 85 or more (`src/server/banking.ts:220-276`; `worker/index.ts:397`). Invoice matching reads `customerName` and `totalAmount`, fields `mapInvoice` never returns, and does not exclude paid or void invoices (`src/server/banking.ts:200-202` against `src/server/invoices.ts:63-87`). The repair routes every match that touches 1100 or 2000 through the existing `receive_invoice_payment` and `pay_bill` RPCs, adds a `reverse_payment` RPC, flags control accounts so manual journals cannot hit them, switches auto-posting off until matching is reference-based, and runs a nightly subledger-versus-GL check that alerts on any difference.

**Unpaginated reads silently truncate.** Supabase caps each API response at the project's max-rows setting, 1,000 by default (background knowledge per the [supabase-js select reference](https://supabase.com/docs/reference/javascript/select); the live setting was not checked). `src/server/reports.ts:15-34` already pages in 1,000-row chunks, so the cap is known, yet `AccountService.getAccountBalances`, which feeds chart-of-accounts balances, budget "spent" and the reconciliation GL balance, does not page (`src/server/accounts.ts:164-188`). Neither does `LedgerService.getJournalEntries`, which feeds the journal list, the dashboard and the Settings "export general ledger" CSV (`src/server/ledger.ts:37-48`), nor the dashboard metrics or the invoice, bill, customer and bank-transaction lists. A company past its first thousand journal lines would hand its auditor an incomplete general-ledger export with nothing to say so. The same design breaks a hard platform ceiling. Workers on the Free plan get **50 external subrequests per invocation** against 10,000 on Paid ([Cloudflare changelog, Feb 2026](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-02-11-subrequests-limit/)); every API call spends two of them on auth before doing work (`worker/auth.ts:45, 68-73`), and the balance sheet pages through every journal line ever posted (`src/server/reports.ts:96-115`), so one balance-sheet request fails at roughly 48,000 lines. Commit `144ee4a` already hit "Too many subrequests" during company creation. Moving to Workers Paid buys time; the durable fix is Postgres functions returning per-account sums (`GROUP BY account_id` with an `entry_date` filter, served by the existing `idx_journal_lines_org_account` and `idx_journal_entries_org_date` indexes), ideally backed by a `gl_balances` table maintained inside `insert_journal_entry`, plus cursor pagination on every list endpoint. Verifying the JWT locally with `getClaims()` instead of calling `auth.getUser()` saves another subrequest per call ([Supabase SSR guide](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/server-side/creating-a-client)).

**The dashboard is wrong before truncation even starts.** "Money in" sums the total of every invoice not marked paid, **including void invoices**, and ignores partial payments; bills repeat the error; "cash position" adds every asset account, including receivables, inventory and recoverable VAT; and monthly trends bucket by month name, merging January 2025 with January 2026 (`src/server/metrics.ts:9-42, 82-123`). Budgets compare spending against lifetime balances rather than the budget period (`src/server/budgets.ts:212-223`). This matters beyond accuracy. An IGC field study in Nairobi found small firms under-invest in records mainly because they cannot get insight back out, and that an app returning automated analytics raised daily record-keeping by **7–9 percentage points** ([IGC](https://www.theigc.org/publications/data-without-insight-why-small-businesses-under-invest-record-keeping-evidence-kenya)). The owner dashboard is the surface that keeps owners recording, and today it misinforms them.

**Six further high-severity gaps complete the list.** The `accountant` role was added in `20260928150000_add_accountant_membership_role.sql`, but the guard every financial RPC calls still admits only owners and admins (`…atomic_financial_workflows.sql:36`), so all eight posting workflows (journals, invoices, bills, both payment types, both voids and payroll) reject the role PRODUCT.md says "posts to the books"; the fix is one `CREATE OR REPLACE`. No lock date or year-end close exists anywhere, so any writer can backdate into any period, reports ignore `fiscal_year_start`, and the balance sheet carries all-time "current-period earnings (unclosed)" instead of closing to retained earnings (`src/utils/reportCalculations.ts:143-149, 250-269`). `OrganizationService.updateOrganization` lets an owner change `base_currency` after posting, silently reinterpreting every historical line (`src/server/organizations.ts:161`). The employee form sends housing and transport allowances, national ID, employment type and M-Pesa number, but `PayrollService.addEmployee` ignores them and no columns exist, so gross pay is base salary only and **PAYE, SHIF and Housing Levy are under-deducted** for anyone with allowances (`src/components/common/DynamicQuickAddModal.tsx:240-256`; `src/server/payroll.ts:110-138, 196`). Foreign-currency payments are applied at the invoice's booked rate, so no realised gain or loss is ever posted as IAS 21 requires; unrealised FX is computed on the fly from free unofficial APIs and never journalised despite PRODUCT.md's claim; rates are stored as foreign-per-KES, the inverse of how Kenyans quote them; and the display-currency switch uses rates held in each browser, so two users can print different "USD" statements from the same books (`…atomic_financial_workflows.sql:936-975`; `src/server/currency.ts:43-298`; `src/utils/currency.ts:110-195`). Finally, VAT is computed in the browser from a free-text rate and the server only checks that tax is non-negative, with no tax codes at all (`src/components/sales/InvoiceBuilder.tsx:117`; `…atomic_financial_workflows.sql:444`).

## Security and quality gaps sit one layer below

**Tenant isolation depends on Worker code, not on the database.** The Worker verifies the Supabase JWT and membership on every `/api` request, then runs every query with the secret key (`src/server/supabase.ts:10-20`), so row-level security never applies to application traffic and isolation rests on each service remembering `.eq('org_id', …)`. Supabase's recommended pattern is a request-scoped client carrying the user's token, which makes RLS a real second line of defence while keeping the service role only for the workflow RPCs ([Supabase SSR guide](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/server-side/creating-a-client)). The same guide says legacy `service_role` keys work only "until the end of 2026". The code already prefers `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY` (`src/server/supabase.ts:6`), but the README still tells operators to set `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` (`README.md:131`), so deploy secrets need changing before January.

**The cheapest high-value security fix is a headers file.** `wrangler.jsonc:8-11` runs the Worker first only for `/api/*`, so `index.html` and every asset come straight from Static Assets, and the CSP, frame and HSTS headers set in Hono (`worker/index.ts:38-55`) never reach the app shell. The SPA is open to clickjacking, and its CSP covers only JSON responses. A `public/_headers` file fixes this, since Static Assets supports up to 100 header rules ([Workers limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/)); the inline script at `index.html:36` needs a CSP hash, and self-hosting the Google Fonts would simplify the policy while removing a render-blocking third-party fetch. Validation is the next layer. zod covers only invoices, bills, payments, journals, bulk operations and onboarding (`worker/index.ts:92-176`); about twenty other write routes, from team and accounts to employees, payroll runs, organisations and currency, pass raw bodies to services typed `input: any`. In practice `base_salary` accepts any JSON value, currency is free text, `manager_id` accepts any auth user, and receipt images are unchecked for size or type. Route-level `@hono/zod-validator` with schemas shared with the client forms is the same work as spec item 3.9 (react-hook-form with zod), and it also stops the silent payroll field drops.

**Account security sits below the finance norm.** There is no MFA and no password reset anywhere in `src/` (`src/context/AuthProvider.tsx:83-105`). TOTP as a second factor, enforced for owners, admins and accountants by checking `aal2` in `worker/auth.ts`, layers on top of email and password and so stays within PRODUCT.md's "email and password only" rule. Passkeys are now mainstream in finance, and NIST SP 800-63-4 requires AAL2 to offer a phishing-resistant option ([Authsignal](https://www.authsignal.com/blog/articles/passwordless-authentication-in-2025-the-year-passkeys-went-mainstream)), but they change the sign-in method, so adopting them is a product-owner decision rather than a default. Password recovery should end with the user setting a password, not become a passwordless sign-in path that brings magic links back by another door. Around these sit smaller gaps. Nothing is rate-limited, leaving Gemini OCR and Q&A, invite emails, organisation creation and forced FX refreshes open to cost abuse. `addMember` pages through **every user in the Supabase project, across all tenants**, to find an email, then attaches an existing user without an acceptance step (`src/server/team.ts:74-107`). The read-only `member` role can read `/employees`, including salaries, KRA PINs and bank accounts. CORS admits requests with no Origin and falls back to localhost. Database error messages reach clients as 400s, and CSV exports do not neutralise leading `= + - @`.

**Ledger immutability is a policy, not a guarantee.** The immutability migration only drops or denies RLS UPDATE and DELETE, which `service_role` bypasses, and the generic policy loop even created an UPDATE policy on `audit_logs` (`…003_rls_policies.sql:262-287`). No trigger stops changes to posted journals, payments, payslips or audit rows, so a Worker bug or leaked secret can rewrite history. Application-level audit writes are fire-and-forget, and employee salary and bank-detail changes are not audited at all (`src/server/audit.ts:4-15`). India's company rules show where auditors' expectations are heading: accounting software there must keep an edit log of every change that **cannot be disabled** ([Tally Solutions](https://tallysolutions.com/accounting/audit-trail-accounting-software-compliance/)). That is not Kenyan law, but it is a credible bar to meet with raise-on-modify triggers, revoked service-role UPDATE and DELETE, and a per-organisation hash chain later.

**Nothing checks a change before merge.** `npm run lint`, which is just `tsc --noEmit`, **fails with 21 errors** because `@types/react` is missing; `strict` is off; and there are 308 uses of `any`. There is no `.github/` directory and therefore no CI, although seven PRs have been merged, and the 48 passing tests cover only pure utilities, with none for the roughly 3,700 lines of Worker and service code or the 3,400 lines of SQL. The broken accountant role is exactly the regression CI plus pgTAP would have caught. A one-day PR that adds the React type packages, fixes the 21 errors and adds a GitHub Actions workflow running `tsc`, `npm test`, `vite build` and `supabase test db` closes this gap.

**Performance and offline behaviour work against PRODUCT.md's three-second mobile target.** The eager shell is about **864 KB raw and 248 KB gzipped**, and the Reports chunk weighs 460 KB because six report views import jsPDF statically; a dynamic import on the export click saves about 150 KB gzipped. TanStack Query runs with `staleTime: 0` and refetches on focus (`src/main.tsx:29`), so every tab switch re-downloads the ledger. Kenyan mobile data costs **USD 0.84–2.25 per GB** ([Kenyan Wall Street](https://kenyanwallstreet.com/expensive-mobile-data-is-stalling-africas-digital-leap-world-bank)) and Android holds about 92% of mobile OS share ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/kenya)), so payload weight matters. There is no service worker, yet the offline banner promises changes "will not reach the books until the connection returns" while nothing actually queues them (`src/components/layout/OfflineBanner.tsx:26`). With power cuts averaging **8.39 hours a month** ([People Daily](https://peopledaily.digital/business/inside-kenyas-power-crisis-as-outages-rise-and-losses-grow/amp)), users will test that promise.

**PRODUCT.md and the interface claim things the code does not do**, which breaks the product's own "say only what is true" principle. PRODUCT.md lists recurring invoices as built (there is no code), cites a `GET /api/public/demo-balance-sheet` route that does not exist, and claims "unrealised-FX journals" and an "eTIMS Type C draft" when `src/server/etims.ts:8-16` only writes a `NOT_CONFIGURED` row. It simultaneously lists effective-dated rate tables, atomic payroll and mobile bottom navigation as unbuilt when all three exist. The bank matcher is labelled AI, and Swahili exists only in the onboarding tour, although PRODUCT.md requires it for the sidebar, primary buttons and empty states. Stale claims spread: the competitor research behind this report took PRODUCT.md at its word and credited Ledger Link with working unrealised FX and AI matching, both of which the audit disproves. Correcting the document and the interface copy is an S-sized task for the first sprint.

## Rivals already sell eTIMS plus M-Pesa, so compete on depth

**Kenyan regulation has turned eTIMS from an invoicing chore into the evidence base for every tax return.** Expenses not backed by an eTIMS invoice have been non-deductible under ITA s.16(1)(c) since 1 January 2024, and since 1 January 2026 KRA has validated the income and expenses declared in returns against eTIMS invoices, withholding-tax certificates and customs import records ([EY](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2025-2471-kenya-revenue-authority-to-validate-income-and-expenses-in-income-tax-returns)). KRA also pre-populates the VAT return from the filer's eTIMS sales and from **suppliers'** eTIMS submissions, so a supplier that never transmits a compliant invoice effectively erases the buyer's input-VAT claim ([KRA FAQ](https://www.kra.go.ke/helping-tax-payers/faqs/the-vat-auto-populated-return)). The Finance Act 2026 added minimum penalties of **KES 100,000 for companies and KES 10,000 for individuals** that fail to use eTIMS ([Business Daily](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/outage-on-kra-s-etims-systems-5536700)), and **750,915 taxpayers** had onboarded by 30 June 2026 ([People Daily](https://peopledaily.digital/business/kra-posts-record-ksh2-84t-revenue-collection-in-fy2025-26)). The friction is real. Manufacturers report that informal, remote and open-market suppliers cannot issue eTIMS invoices ([KAM](https://kam.co.ke/?p=7252)), the portal suffered two disruptions in one week in July 2026 ([People Daily](https://peopledaily.digital/business/kra-restores-etims-online-portal-after-service-disruption/amp)), and tax experts are publicly urging that deductibility be delinked from eTIMS ([Capital FM](https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/business/2026/01/expert-want-expense-deductibility-delinked-from-etims/)). The valuable job is no longer issuing an eTIMS invoice; it is **making sure every income and expense line will match KRA's data before filing.**

**Issuing eTIMS invoices is already table stakes.** Zoho Books Kenya calls itself a KRA-approved eTIMS integrator ([Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/ke/books/etims-compliance)) and takes M-Pesa payments natively ([Zoho help](https://www.zoho.com/ke/books/help/online-payments/m-pesa.html)). A Zoho partner lists Kenyan prices of Free, then KES 999, 1,999 and 2,999 a month (849, 1,699 and 2,499 billed annually), up to Elite at 9,000 and Ultimate at 18,000 ([FNJ Associates](https://fnjassociates.co.ke/?p=2755)). Zoho reports 55% revenue growth in Kenya and in July 2026 partnered with KNCCI to give members KES 65,000 in credits plus onboarding ([Citizen Digital](https://citizen.digital/article/kenyan-smes-to-receive-ksh65k-digital-credits-in-zoho-kncci-partnership-n386629)). Odoo's Kenya localisation auto-installs a native eTIMS OSCU module ([Odoo docs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/finance/fiscal_localizations/kenya.html)). QuickBooks Online has no native eTIMS, and Intuit's own community answer sends Kenyan users to third parties ([QuickBooks Community](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/community/other-questions-88/etims-invoicing-kenya-376172)), which makes its users switchable. ZYNO Books, Qwan, LedgerFlow, Veira, Cute Profit and the longer-running Uhasibu all describe themselves as eTIMS-plus-M-Pesa products, none with published traction ([LedgerFlow](https://ledgerflow.medici.africa/); [Veira](https://veirahq.com/blog/erp-software-kenya)). Bumpa entered Kenya in June 2026 with WhatsApp commerce, M-Pesa collections and bookkeeping for more than 136,000 businesses across Nigeria and Kenya ([Capital FM](https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/business/2026/06/bumpa-enters-kenya-to-tap-growing-sme-digital-commerce-market/)), and Safaricom's free M-PESA for Business app already gives merchants exportable statements and money-in/money-out charts ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.safaricom.mpesa.orgapp&hl=en)). What these rivals leave open are depth gaps: purchase-side eTIMS validation, live M-Pesa ingestion rather than CSV uploads, Kenyan statutory payroll bundled with the ledger (Zoho sells payroll separately), and multi-client workflows for accountants.

**Globally, table stakes sit at the "money in, money out" edges, where Ledger Link is thinnest.** Mid-tier Xero, QuickBooks, Zoho and Sage plans now include quotes, purchase orders, credit notes or vendor credits, online "Pay Now" on invoices or a client portal, live bank feeds with rules, fixed assets, expense claims, receipt capture and a seat for the accountant ([Xero features](https://www.xero.com/uk/accounting-software/all-features/); [Costbench on Zoho Free](https://costbench.com/software/accounting/zoho-books/free-plan/)). Ledger Link already has inventory with COGS, projects and time, budgets and statutory payroll, which incumbents often reserve for upper tiers, but lacks estimates, credit notes, payment links and any transaction feed. Payment links pay off. Older, undated Xero data shows invoices with an online payment option paid **55% sooner**, in 12 days instead of 22 ([Xero](https://blog.xero.com/data-insights/small-business-insights-data-late-payment-results/)), and Intuit reports AI-drafted reminders get businesses paid four days faster on average, a vendor figure ([QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/payments-agent/)). Kenyan conditions make receivables acute: government pending bills reached **KES 465.87 billion** in March 2026, with suppliers waiting 30–90 days and often more than a year ([The Star](https://www.the-star.co.ke/business/2026-07-27-kenyas-sh465-billion-pending-bills-push-cash-strapped-smes-to-alternative-trade-finance)).

**The AI lesson is "deterministic first, supervised always."** Every incumbent shipped agents in 2025–26, from QuickBooks' July 2025 launch to Xero JAX's document chasing and bill screening for changed bank details and Sage Copilot in every tier ([Accounting Today](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/xerocon-2026-jax-ai-improvements-focused-on-automating-unbillable-admin-work)). Practitioners call them "useful but supervised" and report weekly clean-up of miscategorised transactions ([Books LA](https://www.booksla.com/quickbooks-ai-agents/)). The systems that publish high accuracy put rules and predictive models first: Digits says it stops LLMs from doing calculations ([Accounting Today](https://accountingtoday.com/news/digits-says-its-new-ai-agents-can-automate-95-of-bookkeeping-tasks)), and Ramp escalates only the 10–15% of items that need judgement ([CPA Practice Advisor](https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2025/07/10/ramp-introduces-ai-agents-suited-for-controllers/164684/)). Ledger Link's matcher is unsupervised keyword matching that auto-posts, the opposite pattern. PRODUCT.md also fixes the stack at **"Gemini for receipt OCR only"**, yet `/ai/ask` already sends ledger snapshots to Gemini for Q&A (`src/server/aiInsights.ts:149`), so the owner should decide explicitly whether to widen that constraint. Either way, the highest-value AI work fits inside it: deterministic matching on payment references, vendor-to-account rules learned from user corrections, and receipt OCR that also extracts the supplier's eTIMS invoice number and QR code, which turns the existing scanner into a deductibility tool. Xero, Intuit and Zoho now ship official MCP servers or assistant connectors ([Xero MCP server](https://github.com/XeroAPI/xero-mcp-server); [Zoho Books AI](https://www.zoho.com/books/help/ai-features/ai-features.html)), and a read-only Ledger Link equivalent becomes cheap once reports run in SQL.

**Accountants are the distribution channel, which makes the one-line accountant fix commercially urgent.** ICPAK reports more than 40,000 members ([ICPAK](https://icpak.com/)), and Kenyan CPA firms already sell packaged Zoho Books implementations ([FNJ Associates](https://fnjassociates.co.ke/?p=2766)). Intuit is turning month-end close into a paid product: Books Close standardises close across all of a firm's clients with preparer, reviewer and approver roles, and costs **US$8 per client per month (US$6 above 50 clients) from January 2027** ([QuickBooks IAS pricing](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/accountants/pricing/)). Small-firm churn favours the channel too, since 28% of businesses re-contacted in the CBK/KNBS/FSD tracker had closed between survey waves ([KNBS](https://statistics.knbs.or.ke/nada/index.php/catalog/129)), and an accountant's multi-client account outlives any single shop. On price, PRODUCT.md marks the KES 1,500 / 4,500 / 12,000 placeholders as unconfirmed, and they sit above Zoho's equivalent tiers while Kidaftari charges KES 1,000 a month ([MIT Solve](https://solve.mit.edu/solutions/70395)). The evidence favours per-organisation pricing with unlimited users and a free accountant seat, a free or micro tier that includes CSV M-Pesa import, live feeds on paid tiers (Wave's 2026 move of bank feeds to its paid plan shows they can carry a price, per [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/software/reviews/wave-accounting)), and billing through M-Pesa rather than cards. Kippa's collapse left Nigerian merchants locked out of their own invoice and debtor data ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2024/02/23/kippa-users-left-in-the-dark/)), so one-click full export is a trust feature worth advertising, and it is true the day it ships.

## The roadmap: right books now, Kenyan rails next, reach later

The phases follow dependencies rather than wish lists. **Now** means the integrity and security fixes above plus six items that are cheap, legally urgent or time-boxed by known 2027 rule changes. **Next** spans Q4 2026 to mid-2027 and makes Ledger Link a credible system of record for a VAT-registered Kenyan SME. **Later** builds differentiation and reach. Rows within each table are in priority order.

### Now: finish the fixes, feed the ledger, close legal exposure

The banking module, reconciliation report and rules engine all exist but have **no data source**: `syncTransactions` returns "Bank sync isn't connected yet", and the only writer of `bank_transactions` is the demo seed script (`src/server/banking.ts:45-48`; `scripts/seed-demo-org.ts:206`). The audit ranks statement import as the most valuable unbuilt item. Kenyan banks deliver CSV rather than live feeds, and M-Pesa business statements export as CSV with Receipt No, Completion Time, Details, Transaction Status, Paid In, Withdrawn and Balance columns ([Kolonell](https://kolonell.com/en/blog/mpesa-statement-export-accountants-dar-es-salaam-2026); a secondary source, so check real samples). One schema change gates the whole "M-Pesa as a payment rail" position: `bank_transactions` has no `account_id` (`…002_business_tables.sql:92-103`), so M-Pesa clearing account 1050 cannot be reconciled apart from bank 1000. Payroll has two clocks running. The Finance Act 2025 has required employers since 1 July 2025 to "grant an employee all applicable deductions, reliefs and exemptions" ([payroll.org](https://payroll.org/news-resources/news/news-detail/2025/05/12/kenya-mandates-automatic-paye-tax-relief-starting-1-july-2025)), yet Ledger Link still lists pension above NSSF, mortgage interest, PRMF and non-cash benefits as unsupported (`src/utils/kenyaPayroll.ts:52-58`). And a Treasury PAYE-relief proposal (exempt up to KES 30,000 a month, 25% from 30,000 to 50,000) entered public participation in October 2026 with a possible 1 January 2027 start ([Kenyans.co.ke](https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127103-treasury-delays-paye-relief-bill-again-sets-october-timeline)), while NSSF Year 5 limits are due in February 2027. Under the current pay-date lookup, a January payroll paid on 2 February would pick up the new NSSF year (`src/utils/kenyaPayroll.ts:135-143`).

### Next: become the system of record for VAT-registered SMEs (Q4 2026 to mid-2027)

**Sales-side eTIMS is the largest and least certain build, so it starts first.** KRA tests and certifies third-party integrators for OSCU (always-online) and VSCU (bulk or intermittently connected) integration ([KRA](https://www.kra.go.ke/business/etims-electronic-tax-invoice-management-system/learn-about-etims/etims-system-to-system-integration)) and publishes an approved list, most recently dated 26 January 2026 ([KRA list](https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/List-of-Approved-eTIMS-3rd-party-integrators-as-at-26th-January-2026.pdf)). No research stream reached the certification steps, lead time or API specification, so the first action is a conversation with KRA or with a certified integrator about partnership, the route Sage takes through Advatech. Build it behind a country-agnostic fiscalisation adapter (prepare, sign or submit, store the authority's CU number and QR, handle rejection and retry) so that Uganda's EFRIS, Tanzania's VFD and Rwanda's EBM can plug in later, and queue submissions with idempotency keys and a proof-of-attempt record so KRA outages never block invoicing. Until certification lands, let users record the invoice numbers eTIMS Client or eTIMS Lite produced, which keeps their books reconcilable without claiming an integration that does not exist.

**Live M-Pesa comes from three Daraja pieces working together.** Daraja 3.0, launched in November 2025, carries about **25% of all M-Pesa transactions** ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2025/11/25/safaricom-overhauls-m-pesa-api-platform/)), and API access is free beyond Safaricom's tariffs ([HelloDuty](https://helloduty.com/blogs/what-is-a-daraja-api)). The C2B confirmation webhook stores each callback idempotently by `TransID`; the Pull Transactions API re-fetches the last 48 hours of C2B transactions to recover missed callbacks ([Daraja v3 docs mirror](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/@JacksCodeVault/mpesa-daraja-mcp/blob/e875f3b350a0119c928c623c4e0cdd8999a5d329/daraja_docs_v3/docs/PullTransaction.md)); and STK Push on invoices sends the invoice number as `AccountReference`, so payments match deterministically instead of by guesswork. The friction is onboarding, because each merchant needs its own active Paybill or Till, a signed go-live letter and whitelisted server IPs ([DEV.to guide](https://dev.to/msnmongare/how-to-go-live-with-m-pesa-daraja-api-production-environment-4h96)), so smaller merchants need an aggregator route: IntaSend charges 1% ([IntaSend](https://support.intasend.com/portal/en/kb/articles/how-do-you-charge-for-the-service)), Paystack 1.5% ([Paystack](https://paystack.com/ke/pricing)), and Kopo Kopo's K2 Connect explicitly targets accounting-package integration ([Kopo Kopo](https://kopokopo.co.ke/introducing-k2connect/)). Fee auto-posting must follow the **August 2026 tariff**, under which Buy Goods is free to KES 500 and then 0.55% capped at KES 200 ([The Kenya Times](https://thekenyatimes.com/business/safaricom-reduces-m-pesa-business-charges-list-of-new-charges/)), stored as dated data rather than constants.

### Later: differentiate on WhatsApp, lending and accountants' AI tools (from mid-2027)

WhatsApp invoicing waits for STK Push and C2B reconciliation, which is why it sits here even though PRODUCT.md headlines it. Meta has billed per delivered template since 1 July 2025 ([Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing)), and since 1 October 2026 service messages inside the 24-hour window are also charged beyond **1,000 free a month per number** ([respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-pricing-change-2026.md)). Secondary trackers put Kenya at about **US$0.004 per utility message and US$0.0225 per marketing message** ([EngageLab](https://www.engagelab.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing)), so invoices, receipts and reminders should be written as transactional utility templates and WhatsApp costs metered per plan. Bumpa's arrival argues for positioning this as real books for businesses that sell on WhatsApp, not a storefront. Lending partnerships follow the money: only about **16% of Kenyan MSMEs** reach formal credit ([The Star](https://www.the-star.co.ke/business/kenya/2024-06-07-kenyas-small-ventures-lack-formal-credit-access--survey)), and India's strongest SME-credit gains came from pairing tax-invoice (GST) data with bank data in its Account Aggregator system ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/account-aggregator-ecosystem-driving-credit-access-says-finance-ministry-125090200268_1.html)). A ledger holding eTIMS-validated invoices alongside M-Pesa flows is the Kenyan equivalent, and the realistic path for a small team is a lender pack plus referral partnerships, not a credit licence.

## Values to verify before they become code

Every external research stream reported WebFetch blocked for nearly every domain, including kra.go.ke, kenyalaw.org, developers.facebook.com and the vendor sites, and each exhausted its search budget, so the findings above rest on search-engine summaries of the cited pages rather than full-text reads. The code audit is primary evidence because it read the repository, but it did not execute SQL. Several figures conflict between sources, and the table below says where.

**The most dangerous open question is the deadline roll rule.** `src/utils/statutory.ts` moves a deadline that falls on a weekend to the next working day, but the regulation notes say some KRA guidance is believed to use the previous working day; if so, Ledger Link would show some deadlines a day late and its users would carry the penalty. Confirm it with KRA before the calendar gains holiday support. Data protection needs the same care: ODPC's April 2026 draft guidance treats storing Kenyan personal data on a foreign cloud as a cross-border transfer requiring a documented basis ([ITWeb](https://itweb.africa/article/kenya-tightens-cross-border-data-transfer-rules/WnpNgq21y6kMVrGd)), which covers both Supabase hosting and receipts sent to Gemini, and PRODUCT.md lists the ODPC registration number as pending, so no page may imply registration.

## Conclusion

Ledger Link's strongest asset is also its blind spot. The Postgres core really does refuse unbalanced books, which is more than many rivals can demonstrate, and that strength makes it easy to assume the numbers are right when the audit shows they leak at every edge where money arrives, gets matched or gets summarised. That makes trust the cheapest advantage to recover: the integrity fixes cost days rather than quarters, and two of them, SQL-side aggregation and the accountant guard, also clear the Workers subrequest ceiling and reopen the accountant sales channel. The 2026 regulatory shift redefines what "Kenyan-first" should mean. When KRA tests every claimed expense against eTIMS, a ledger that defends a customer's deductions is worth more than one that merely issues invoices, and Ledger Link's existing bill workflow, receipt scanner and audit log give it an unusually direct path to that job. Two external clocks set the sequence: rate-table and pay-period work must land before the PAYE and NSSF changes of January and February 2027, and the comment window on the payments bill that could make bank and M-Pesa data portable to accounting software closes on 9 October 2026.
